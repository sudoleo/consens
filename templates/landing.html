02 · Run

The agent shows its work.

It makes a plan, searches the web, calculates in its own workspace and hands you real files: a chart in the answer, a spreadsheet you open beside the chat. The AIs you chose follow every step and review the answer independently, without ever steering it. Before a risky step, one of them can pause the run until you decide. Scroll to move the run.

03 · Decide

The doubt is marked in the answer.

One answer, read top to bottom, with the reviewers' doubts written into it rather than hidden behind it. Four things carry that.

  1. 01

    Who checked it

    Under every answer stand the AIs that reviewed it and what they found. There is no score: a passage carries a reviewer's note, or nobody objected to it. Unmarked text means no objection, not confirmed.

    {% for name, icon, mono in [('Claude', 'claude.png', false), ('DeepSeek', 'deepseek.png', false)] %} {% endfor %} Reviewed by Claude, DeepSeek

    1 wrong · 1 doubtful · 1 incomplete

    Unmarked text means no objection, not confirmed.

    Notes 3 Answers 2

  2. 02

    What a reviewer noted

    Click a marked passage and its notes open: who noted it, what kind of note it is, and in what words, with the source where the reviewer gave one.

    Try the marked sentence.

    Purchase costs: 3.5% property transfer tax and 2% for notary and land registry, €17,050 in total.

    Wrong: 1 reviewer objects — open details

    Wrong: 1 reviewer objects 3.5% property transfer tax
    Claude · Wrong
    Saxony has charged 5.5% since January 2023. That makes the purchase costs €23,250, and buying only pulls ahead in year 13, after the 12 years you plan to stay.

  3. 03

    What the colours mean

    A noted passage wears the colour of its most serious note: red where a reviewer says it is wrong, amber where one doubts it, grey where something is missing. The mark sits on the sentence it belongs to, so you never have to hold two documents open at once.

    • 3.5% property transfer tax red · wrong
    • The flat gains 1.5% in value a year amber · doubtful
    • The rate stays at 3.6% until the loan is repaid grey · incomplete
    • As tenants you pay €925 rent unmarked · no objection

  4. 04

    What the others answered

    When independent views help, or when you ask for them, the agent puts its question to the same AIs. Each answers on its own, and under Answers you read every answer in full, or two side by side.

    • Claude If the 12 years are firm, buying is likely the better deal, but not by much, and not for the reason most people think.
    • DeepSeek If you are not sure you will really stay 12 years, I would keep renting.
