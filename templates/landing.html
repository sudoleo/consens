03 · Decide

The doubt is marked in the answer.

One answer, read top to bottom — with the disagreement written into it rather than hidden behind it. Four things carry that, and you can try each one here.

  1. 01

    How far apart they were

    Every checked answer carries one number: how much the six answers actually agreed. An uninvolved model works it out, so the model that wrote the answer is not also grading it.

    45/100 Partial agreement 1 critical · 1 minor detail · disputed: keep or cut the line asking if the delay is a problem, 6 models compared Analysis by Gemini independent of the consensus engine

  2. 02

    Who backed each claim

    The small number behind a sentence is how many models supported it. Rest on it, or tap it, and it names them: who agreed, who deviated, and in what words.

    Try it on the 4/6.

    The new date belongs in the first line, ahead of the miss, the cause and the apology

    Name the day you will confirm the 29th, so the next update does not arrive as another surprise

  3. 03

    Where they pull apart

    Every checked sentence is highlighted in the colour of its result: green where every model agreed, amber where they split, red where they contradict each other. The mark sits on the sentence it belongs to, so you never have to hold two documents open at once.

    • Say what she actually gets on the 15th6/6 green · all of them agreed
    • Name the day you will confirm the 29th4/6 amber · support is split
    • Name the cause, or leave it vague grey · a difference in detail
    • Keep the closing question, or cut it red · they contradict each other

  4. 04

    What they actually said

    Click a mark and the disagreement opens: both camps, named, with the sentence each side actually wrote and a note on what is worth checking yourself.

    Try the marked sentence.

    Three models read the closing line as the only sentence that gives her a way in, three as an invitation to reopen a date you cannot move.

    Contradiction · critical Keep or cut the line asking if the delay is a problem?
    ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok
    Keep it. It is the only opening the message offers her.
    a delay announced without one reads like a decision taken for her
    Claude, Mistral, DeepSeek
    Cut it. It asks her to rule on something you cannot change.
    the honest answer to that question is yes, it is a problem
    Worth verifying: decide first whether the new date is still negotiable. If it is not, do not ask a question that implies it is.
