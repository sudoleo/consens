One prompt, several independent answers

Single-model chat can be fast, but it hides uncertainty. consens.io is built for prompts where you want to compare ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, Kimi, GLM, Meta Muse, and Qwen — up to six families per run — before deciding which answer is useful.

What you can compare

  • Answer quality: see which model gives the clearest, most useful response.
  • Reasoning differences: inspect where models disagree or make different assumptions.
  • Coverage gaps: catch missing context that one model may skip.
  • Consensus: combine common ground and useful complementary details while retaining caveats.

Choose how much to inspect

Start with Daily, Open Models or High Quality, or choose models through Custom. In Consensus mode, the app builds the consensus automatically; Compare keeps the answers separate. Open Model answers for the originals and a two-answer comparison; inspect Differences for opposing positions.

The agreement score measures model agreement, not the probability of being correct.

When AI model comparison helps

Use it for research questions, writing drafts, coding approaches, decision support, study notes, and any prompt where a single confident answer is not enough. The goal is not to declare one model universally best, but to make differences visible before you rely on the output.

FAQ

What is an AI model comparison tool?

It sends the same prompt to multiple AI models and lets you compare the original answers side by side.

Which AI models can consens.io compare?

consens.io is built around major providers including OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, Moonshot AI Kimi, Z.ai GLM, Meta Muse, and Alibaba Qwen. The exact model line-up can change over time.

Why compare several AI models?

Comparing several models helps reveal agreement, disagreement, missing context, and claims that should be checked before relying on a single answer.
