One AI works, others check it

Single-model chat can be fast, but it hides uncertainty. On consens.io you pick one of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, Kimi, GLM, Meta Muse, Qwen and MiMo to do the work, and others to check it as it goes.

What you can compare

  • Answer quality: see which model gives the clearest, most useful response.
  • Reasoning differences: inspect where models disagree or make different assumptions.
  • Coverage gaps: catch missing context that one model may skip.
  • Doubts in place: see which sentences of the answer a reviewer finds wrong, doubtful or incomplete.

Choose how much to inspect

Presets choose the reviewers: Daily (the default, quick), Open Models (open-weight reviewers) or High Quality (Pro, the strongest models), or pick them yourself. The agent writes the answer, and the reviewers mark sentences they find wrong, doubtful or incomplete. Open Answers for the answers of the models the agent asked, or compare two side by side; a plain side-by-side comparison waits in the (+) menu.

Older public answers in the Consensus Library still show the agreement score of the consensus engine; it measures how far the models agreed, not the probability of being correct.

When AI model comparison helps

Use it for research questions, writing drafts, coding approaches, decision support, study notes, and any prompt where a single confident answer is not enough. The goal is not to declare one model universally best, but to make differences visible before you rely on the output.

FAQ

What is an AI model comparison tool?

It sends the same prompt to multiple AI models and lets you compare the original answers side by side. On consens.io the agent can ask other models its question, and you read their answers in full or two side by side.

Which AI models can consens.io compare?

consens.io is built around major providers including OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, Moonshot AI Kimi, Z.ai GLM, Meta Muse, Alibaba Qwen, and Xiaomi MiMo. The exact model line-up can change over time.

Why compare several AI models?

Comparing several models helps reveal agreement, disagreement, missing context, and claims that should be checked before relying on a single answer.
