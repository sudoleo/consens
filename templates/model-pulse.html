    How to read it

    01

    A rate, not a vote count

    A family that is in more runs collects more picks without being any better. So each family's picks are divided by the runs it was in: 30 picks from 100 runs is a 30% best-answer rate.

    02

    The tick is chance

    With six answers in a run, a blind draw would pick each one about 17% of the time. The tick on every bar marks that fair share for the runs the family was in. "2.8× fair share" means picked 2.8 times as often as chance.

    03

    Wide band, few runs

    The shaded band is the range the true rate plausibly lies in. Families under {{ pulse.min_runs if pulse else 10 }} runs are listed but not ranked.

    04

    Pick a rival

    "Only runs with" narrows the board to runs that included one family and shows each other family's head-to-head score against it.

    05

    Honest limits

    The questions are real and uncontrolled, and the pick is a judgment, not a verified answer key. This is not a popularity vote and not an accuracy benchmark.
