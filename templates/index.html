      How it works

      It shapes how you are answered, never what is true — where your memory and the evidence disagree, the evidence wins. It is stored on your account, reaches every device, and is deleted with your account. Watch reruns never use it.

      Nothing is collected automatically unless you switch on Let Agent update memory. Switch it off and nothing new is saved; what was saved stays until you delete it here.

      Help

      {#- What the product is today (Apple-Audit H7): one answer that several models check, Watches, the allowance, memory, data. Six questions, short, in the words the app uses. -#}

      consens.io answers your question with one AI, and AI models from other companies review its work and its answer, so you see where they object.

      1. What happens when I send a message? ＋

      Agent works on your question: it searches, reads sources and, where it helps, asks the models you chose for their own independent answers. The same models are its reviewers: they follow every step and review the finished answer. The models come from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Mistral, Grok, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI/Kimi, Z.ai/GLM, Meta Muse, Alibaba Qwen and Xiaomi MiMo. While it works, the line above the answer shows each step; open it later to see what was done. Compare (the answers side by side), files and Reasoning are in the (+) menu next to the message box.

      2. What do the marks in an answer mean? ＋

      Once the answer is written, your reviewers check it sentence by sentence. A mark shows where one of them objects: red where a statement is wrong, amber where it is doubtful, grey where something is missing. Click a mark to see who said what and why. Unmarked text means no reviewer objected, not that it is proven. Older answers show the earlier check instead: where the model answers differed, with an agreement score. Settings › Display decides what is marked.

      3. What is a Watch? ＋

      A Watch asks your question again on a schedule, weekly or monthly (daily on Pro). It writes to you by email or Telegram only when new evidence moves the answer, or when what you are waiting for happens. Start one with the Watch button under an answer; all of them are under Watches at the top.

      4. How much can I use it? ＋

      consens.io is free while it’s being tested. Sending needs an account. Every account has a daily allowance that all messages share; the ring at the bottom of the sidebar shows what’s left and when it resets (midnight UTC). More models, Reasoning and longer answers use more of it. With your own OpenRouter API key (Settings › Connections), messages run on your key instead.

      5. What does consens.io remember? ＋

      Your chats are saved to your account, so you can open and continue them. Memory (Settings › Memory) holds what you tell it about yourself and goes with your questions while it’s on; you can edit, pause or delete it. Agent adds details from your messages only if you turn on “Let Agent update memory”.

      6. What happens to my data? ＋

      Your messages go through OpenRouter to the providers whose models answer them, and only to endpoints that don’t collect prompts for training. Delete a chat from its menu in the sidebar; deleting your account (Settings › Account) removes your chats and everything stored about you. The privacy notice has the details; questions go to contact@consens.io.
